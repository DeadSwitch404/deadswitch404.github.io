Whisper Engine - Retired & Reborn

The World Is Changing - So We Are

Tools that once served us - now they can hold us back.
This is why silentarchitect.org is retired in its current form.

DeadSwitch has reached many people.

  • I got messages, more kudos and some threats.
  • I met with sceptics and many followers.
  • I signaled a clean, strong message.

We -architects- must think in systems.
We must reduce complexity.

Retired

Whisper Engine is the Org Mode-based Emacs Lisp static site generator.

  • It was the signal for many of you to learn system architecture.
  • The tool matured.
  • Then maturity and complexity moved together.

As Whisper Engine exists today -the long Emacs Lisp code- is no longer maintainable.

Whisper Engine v1.1.2 is the latest release.

Retired.

Reborn

Whisper Engine as a small and low footprint system is reborn.

The foundation remains Emacs.
The power comes from Org Mode.

Whisper Engine v2.0 is a systems design, an architecture around ox-publish.

  • Less complexity.
  • Smaller size.
  • More control.

More details later. A new system is surfacing.

Reborn.

Final Whisper

Whisper Engine doesn't disappear.
The silentarchitect.org site doesn't stop signaling.
